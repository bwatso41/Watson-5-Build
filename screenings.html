Upcoming Screenings

Join the Indie Film Collective for After Hours, a screening series about the places we leave behind. This lineup pairs found-footage horror with documentaries about the people who worked the rides, ran the arcades, and still have the keys.

  • Please Use Other Exit

    A night cleaner props open a stairwell door with her mop bucket. Three floors down, she trips over the same bucket.

  • One Token Left

    Two urban explorers bring a pocketful of tokens to an abandoned boardwalk arcade. The only machine that still works is a photo booth, and somebody has just taken their picture.

  • The Summer We Signed a Waiver

    Former teenage lifeguards narrate home movies from a notorious water park. Halfway through a tape, one of them asks to rewind. That kid on the slide is her dad.

  • Rain Checks Honored

    Years after a hurricane closed a seaside amusement park, its former ticket seller collects unused rain checks from local families. Each one comes with a story about the summer everyone thought they’d be back.
