Meet and Greet

Meet a few of the filmmakers who lead workshops at the Indie Film Collective. Each brings a different approach to storytelling, from finding a documentary subject close to home to bringing a clay character to life one frame at a time.

  1. Nora Ellis - makes short documentaries about family businesses and the people who keep them running.
  2. Theo Park - builds miniature sets and clay characters for stop-motion animation.
  3. Maya Bennett - writes and directs coming-of-age dramas about friendship in small towns.
  4. Julian Reyes - shoots live music performances and directs music videos for local bands.
